Prices
Solar and cloud

Everything runs from now to the end of the third day. "Effective cost" is power bought, minus feed-in earned, plus any battery charge you'd be short at the end compared with the best option.

How it works: for every hour it runs your house. Solar powers the house and car first, then fills the battery, then exports (or is switched off when feed-in is negative). The battery covers the house at night, and the car too if allowed. The grid covers the rest. Spike protection stops the car taking battery energy the house will need to get through a price spike, assuming solar comes in 30% under forecast. The charge limit uses the heat rule: round tomorrow's top temperature to the nearest 10 and take it off 100 (never below 50%).
